My BMX Season

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 July 2026

My BMX Season ("the app") is a personal season-tracking tool for BMX racing schedules, deadlines and club races. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We store:

  • Which race weekends you've marked as "Going"
  • Booking/checklist status you record for each race (e.g. parking, camping, practice booked)
  • Your email notification preference (on/off)
  • Any information you submit through the site's Submit form (e.g. club race details or deadline corrections), which is reviewed before being published

How we use it

This information is used only to run the app: showing your personal season dashboard, tracking your deadlines, and — if you enable it — sending you an email reminder the day before a booking deadline or race. We do not use your data for advertising and do not sell it to third parties.

Third-party services

The app relies on a small number of service providers to operate:

  • Google — for sign-in (authentication only)
  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication
  • Resend — sends transactional reminder emails on our behalf
  • Cloudflare — hosts the website

Each of these providers only receives the minimum data needed to perform their function (e.g. Resend receives your email address only to deliver a reminder message).

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, get in touch via the contact details listed on the My BMX Season website.

Children's privacy

The app itself is intended for use by riders, parents or club administrators managing a race season, not for use directly by young children. Account sign-in requires a Google account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be directed via the contact details listed on the My BMX Season website.
